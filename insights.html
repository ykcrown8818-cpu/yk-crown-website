Real estate · 12 Nov 2026

BUPi Free Exemption Ends: Land Regularisation Can Cost 250 Euros

The exceptional free regime for regularising properties through the Single Land Registry (BUPi) has ended, reports ECO. Landowners who have not yet regularised their rustic or mixed plots may now face costs of up to 250 euros.

Construction · 10 Nov 2026

Portugal new home building costs accelerate to 7.4% in September

Construction costs for new residential properties in Portugal rose by 7.4% year-on-year in September, according to estimates from Statistics Portugal (INE) reported by ECO.

Real estate · 10 Nov 2026

Portugal house price-to-income ratio rose 60.8% in a decade, Eurostat reports

Portugal's house price-to-income ratio jumped by 60.8% between 2015 and 2025, registering the sharpest increase across the European Union, according to Eurostat data reported by RTP Notícias.

Euribor rates fall across three, six and 12-month tenors
