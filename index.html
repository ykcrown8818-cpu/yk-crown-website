YK CROWN · PORTO

Praça da Corujeira

A contemporary residential development shaped by its relationship with architecture, light, landscape and the life of eastern Porto.

Porto · PortugalYK CROWN Development
The Project

Designed around place, proportion and everyday living.

Praça da Corujeira is presented as a complete development story — from its urban setting and architectural expression to the interior atmosphere and material details.

LocationPraça da Corujeira, Campanhã, Porto
FocusContemporary residential design and quality of living
DeveloperYK CROWN, LDA
01 · Location & Surroundings

A green urban setting in eastern Porto.

Praça da Corujeira is in Campanhã, in the eastern part of Porto. The square is known for its mature plane trees and public garden, with areas for sport and leisure. The wider eastern zone is undergoing continued urban renewal and investment.

Location context: Visit Porto and Porto City Council sources. Specific travel times and distances should be verified before publication.

02 · Architecture & Exterior

A calm contemporary façade with strong natural light.

The architectural expression is defined by a clean white façade, generous glazing, balconies and dark metal detailing. The composition creates a restrained contemporary identity while opening the homes towards daylight and the surrounding trees.

Materials & Details

Material expression without excess.

From the street, the project reads through the contrast between light façades, transparent glazing and darker architectural details. The result is contemporary, precise and visually connected to the mature landscape around the building.

Technical material specifications, brands and systems will be published only when confirmed in the project documentation.

03 · Interiors

Warm, bright and considered for everyday life.

The interior language combines a restrained neutral palette with timber accents, soft textiles and carefully framed natural light. Kitchens, living spaces and bedrooms are presented as calm, functional environments rather than decorative showpieces.

04 · Bathrooms

Detail-led bathrooms with a distinctive material palette.

The bathrooms use strong surface texture, transparent shower screens, dark hardware and clean white sanitaryware to create a contemporary, hotel-inspired feel.
